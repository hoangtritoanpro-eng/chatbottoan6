Thay $b = \frac{9}{10}$ vào biểu thức B ta được:

$B = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{10} + \frac{2}{9} \cdot \frac{9}{10} - \frac{9}{10} : \frac{9}{4}$

$= \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{1}{3} + \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{2}{9} - \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{4}{9}$

$= \frac{9}{10} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{9} - \frac{4}{9}\right)$

$= \frac{9}{10} \cdot \left(\frac{1 \cdot 3}{3 \cdot 3} + \frac{2}{9} - \frac{4}{9}\right)$

$= \frac{9}{10} \cdot \left(\frac{3}{9} + \frac{2}{9} - \frac{4}{9}\right)$

$= \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{3 + 2 - 4}{9}$

$= \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{1}{9}$

$= \frac{9 \cdot 1}{10 \cdot 9}$

$= \frac{1}{10}$
