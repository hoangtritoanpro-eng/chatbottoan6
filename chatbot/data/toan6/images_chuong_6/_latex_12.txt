b) $\frac{-3}{8} + \frac{7}{4} - \frac{1}{12} = \frac{(-3) \cdot 3}{8 \cdot 3} + \frac{7 \cdot 6}{4 \cdot 6} - \frac{1 \cdot 2}{12 \cdot 2} = \frac{-9}{24} + \frac{42}{24} - \frac{2}{24} = \frac{-9 + 42 - 2}{24} = \frac{31}{24}$

c) $\frac{3}{5} : \left(\frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{7}{5}\right) = \frac{3}{5} : \frac{1 \cdot 7}{4 \cdot 5} = \frac{3}{5} : \frac{7}{20} = \frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{20}{7} = \frac{3 \cdot 4}{7} = \frac{12}{7}$

d) $\frac{10}{11} + \frac{4}{11} : 4 - \frac{1}{8}$

$= \frac{10}{11} + \frac{4}{11} \cdot \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{10}{11} + \frac{4 \cdot 1}{11 \cdot 4} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{10}{11} + \frac{1}{11} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{10 + 1}{11} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{11}{11} - \frac{1}{8} = 1 - \frac{1}{8}$

$= \frac{8}{8} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{8 - 1}{8} = \frac{7}{8}$

$B = \frac{5}{13} \cdot \frac{8}{15} + \frac{5}{13} \cdot \frac{26}{15} - \frac{5}{13} \cdot \frac{8}{15}$

$= \frac{5}{13} \cdot \left(\frac{8}{15} + \frac{26}{15} - \frac{8}{15}\right)$

$= \frac{5}{13} \cdot \frac{8 + 26 - 8}{15}$

$= \frac{5}{13} \cdot \frac{26}{15}$

$= \frac{5 \cdot 26}{13 \cdot 15}$

$= \frac{2}{3}$
